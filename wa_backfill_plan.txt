# WhatsApp Backfill Plan — Claims updated after July 9, 2026

## Current State
- `WHATSAPP_CUTOFF_DATE = datetime.datetime(2099, 1, 1)` → **ALL messages BLOCKED**
- WA is triggered only on status change via the portal save route
- Claims registered/updated after July 9 **never received** their notification

## What Needs to Happen

### Step 1 — Set the Cutoff Date (Enable WA for new claims)
Change `WHATSAPP_CUTOFF_DATE` to `2026-07-09` so future saves trigger messages for all claims from July 9 onward.

### Step 2 — Backfill Script (Send missed messages)
A one-time script to send WA notifications to claims that:
- Have `synced_at` or `date` > 2026-07-09
- Have a meaningful status (Registered, Repair Completed, Replacement Approved, Rejected)
- Have NOT already received a WA notification (`Last_Notified_Status` is empty or different from current status)

### Templates to Use
| Status               | Template                             | Params               |
|----------------------|--------------------------------------|----------------------|
| REGISTERED           | myg_onsitego_registered_main         | [name, model, sr_no] |
| REPAIR COMPLETED     | myg_onsitego_repair_completed_main   | [name, sr_no]        |
| REPLACEMENT APPROVED | myg_onsitego_replacement_main        | [name, model]        |
| REJECTED             | osg_clm_reject                       | [name]               |

### Step 3 — Safety Controls
- Rate limit: 1 message per second (API limit)
- Dry-run mode: preview list before sending
- Skip if mobile number is invalid/empty
- Skip if `Last_Notified_Status` already matches current status
- Log every send to `whatsapp_backfill_log.txt`

### Backfill Scope (estimated)
Claims updated after July 9 with notifiable status will be queried first (dry-run).
